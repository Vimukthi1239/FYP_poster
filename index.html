1 Abstract

Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) in Sri Lanka's tourism sector—such as boutique villas, eco-lodges, and family-owned guest houses—face severe market barriers due to high Online Travel Agency (OTA) commissions (15-25%), lack of spatial proximity discovery, and unautomated management tools.

Hoteleco-Pro delivers an integrated, zero-commission eco-tourism ecosystem combining a Spatial Machine Learning Recommendation Engine ($O(\log N)$ Haversine BallTree), bespoke trip itinerary generation, live flight integration, and serverless n8n business automations tailored for SME hoteliers.

2 Introduction & SME Focus

While large luxury chains dominate traditional booking channels, Sri Lanka's small and medium eco-villas struggle with operational visibility:

  • OTA Dependency & Margin Drain: High commission rates reduce net profit margins for small resort operators.
  • Spatial Proximity Blindness: Remote eco-villas near natural attractions remain undiscovered without spatial clustering.
  • Transit Price Opacity: Inability to dynamically calculate multi-destination transport costs (Tuk-Tuk, Sedan, SUV, Van).
  • Manual Operations: Lack of automated guest communication, weather marketing, and financial tracking tools.
3 Main Objective/s
  • Democratize AI Proximity Discovery: Build a $O(\log N)$ BallTree K-NN spatial model linking tourists directly to nearby SME eco-villas and attractions.
  • PickATrip Bespoke Planner: Integrate live Amadeus Flight API search and dynamic local vehicle distance pricing equations.
  • Zero-Cost Serverless Automations: Deploy n8n.io workflows for automated email receipts, weather-driven promotions, and PDF financial ledgers.
  • SME Hotelier Dashboard: Provide real-time reservation trackers, occupancy analytics, and review management without middleware overhead.

5 Research Method - Spatial ML & Pricing Model

Proximity matching computes distance on Earth's curved surface using the Haversine Metric built into a binary BallTree index:

$$d = 2r \cdot \arcsin\left(\sqrt{\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \phi}{2}\right) + \cos(\phi_1)\cos(\phi_2)\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \lambda}{2}\right)}\right)$$
  • Spherical Metric ($r = 6371.0\text{ km}$): Prevents planar distortion across Sri Lanka's geography.
  • Thread-Safe Concurrency: Uses Python threading.RLock() to rebuild spatial trees asynchronously without disrupting read requests.
  • Vehicle Route Pricing Equation:
    $$\text{Total Cost} = (\text{Est. Distance} \times \text{Vehicle Rate}) + (\text{Nights} \times \text{Driver Fee})$$

7 Conclusion & Future Work

Hoteleco-Pro empowers Sri Lanka's small and medium-sized hotels, boutique villas, and eco-resorts with cutting-edge spatial AI and serverless automations without expensive intermediary fees.

  • Empowers SME Hoteliers: Provides high-end operational dashboards, automated receipts, and weather marketing at zero extra cost.
  • Optimizes Tourist Journeys: Delivers accurate E2E flight, hotel, and vehicle route pricing.
  • Future Scope: Expand real-time carbon footprint calculation per vehicle route and release native mobile apps (iOS/Android).
